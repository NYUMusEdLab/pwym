Soundbreaking

Soundbreaking is an interactive collaboration with the Rock and Roll Forever Foundation, PBS, and the producers of Soundbreaking, an 8-episode documentary chronicling the art and history of recorded music.

The NYU MusEDLab developed "Tech Tools" for classroom use, complementing lesson plans found at TeachRock.org.
