Listening & attention

Notice a change. Test a judgment. Listen again.

Experimental release

Tuning Partner

Develop tuning by ear with a pure-fifth drone, an adjustable synthetic tone and optional beating support.

Tolerance is adjustable below two cents. Record locally, or try live instrument pitch estimates with optional visual feedback, phrase timing and history. Live tracking is experimental; use headphones with the reference.

Listening Architecture

Mark changes in a recording, annotate moments and passages, and revisit saved listenings in a notebook.

Use your own audio or the optional demo. Export your notebook to keep a portable copy.

Spatial Music Lab

Move individual sound sources to explore a spatial mix, then capture, save and replay movement takes in the Movement Notebook.

Developed from Yiting Liu’s prototype. Explore the project and collaborators →

Experimental app

Spatial Listening Maps

Listen to a song section by section and place the instruments or sounds you hear from left to right and near to far. Add drawings and notes, then compare your maps.

Maps show your listening interpretation. Moving a label does not change the recording. Your audio stays on your device; export maps to keep a portable copy.

Before you begin

Start audio softly. Browser, microphone and touch support vary by tool. Check each tool’s own controls and notes; physical tablet and Safari testing is ongoing.

Many previews keep work in the browser where you made it. Use their export or save controls before switching devices or clearing browser data.
