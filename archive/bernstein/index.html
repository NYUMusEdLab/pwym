Historical study prototype · 2017 source

Bernstein Young People’s Concerts

A study interface for selected Leonard Bernstein Young People’s Concerts, bringing together video, captions, chapter navigation, transcripts and scripts.

Project record

The interface was designed to help users study the relationship between Bernstein’s prepared script and televised delivery. Users can follow captions or musical cues, click transcript passages or chapter entries to move through a concert, and consult the original script alongside the video.

The surviving repository contains ten episode pages, caption and navigation tracks, script PDFs and an Able Player implementation. The latest recorded source revision is dated 21 October 2017.

The original README records unfinished work on search, seeking, script display and volume controls. A local repair candidate now fixes the seek-bar range and adapts the study layout to smaller screens; it has not been deployed.

Credits

  • Video and closed captions: Marcelo Hanta-Davis.
  • Troubleshooting and technical assistance: Kevin Schlottmann, New York Philharmonic Archives.
  • Text editing and proofing: Hannah Edgar.

Availability

Checks on 14 October 2026 confirmed that the historical pages and all ten MP4 endpoints respond. A brief test of Episode 1 verified playback, transcript seeking and chapter navigation. The local repair candidate also passed keyboard seeking and layout checks across ten episodes at desktop, tablet and phone sizes. Other episodes’ playback and physical mobile devices remain untested.

The original notice limits the site to study use and prohibits recirculation. It credits the concert video to The Leonard Bernstein Office Inc., courtesy of Kultur Video. This archive provides no video launch or full-media embed while the permitted scope of access is being clarified.
