Virtual Piano Duet

Improvise a virtual duet with pianist Steve Wogaman of the Chamber Music Society of Detroit. The public repository credits code adapted from Yotam Mann, design by Diana Castro and Alex Ruthmann, and code updates by Guergana Tzatchkova.

Responsive design update: May 2020, according to the public source README.

Virtual Piano Duet remains active. Public source is available; piano interaction and video playback have not been fully retested for this catalog.

MathScienceMusic

A public toolkit connecting music, mathematics and science through learning materials for K–12 and college classrooms. Developed with Herbie Hancock and the Thelonious Monk Institute of Jazz, now the Herbie Hancock Institute of Jazz.

Launched on International Jazz Day in 2016.
