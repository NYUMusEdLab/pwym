Restored legacy app

Puzzle Cards

Reorder graphical music cards to reconstruct a piece or create a remix. Developed with the Chamber Music Society of Detroit and performers including the Aeolus Quartet.

Open restored CMSD Puzzle Cards →

Browse the earlier general Puzzle Cards restoration for its existing repertoire.

CMSD repertoire

  • School Project — Alexandra T. Bryant, performed by the Aeolus String Quartet. This is the artwork version linked by the original project page.
  • Moanin’ — Bobby Timmons, performed by Marcus Elliot and Michael Malis.

Choose Play, Solve or Remix in the app. These launches start from the repertoire rather than an unavailable historical saved session.

What has been tested

Play, Solve and Remix card/audio interactions; saving and reloading new Remix work in the same browser. The CMSD release passed 18 local mode/repertoire checks at desktop and emulated touch-tablet sizes, plus five storage tests. Its five published files match the tested build byte-for-byte. Physical iPad and Safari testing remains outstanding.

Availability and limits

Historical saved sessions have not been recovered. New Remix saves stay in this browser; export a JSON file to keep a separate copy or import it elsewhere. Local session links do not share your work with other people. Paint remains unavailable.

Credits and provenance

This restoration retains the original interactions and links to existing music and artwork. No recordings are included in this profile.
