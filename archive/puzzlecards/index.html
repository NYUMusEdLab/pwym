Restored legacy app

Puzzle Cards

Reorder graphical music cards to reconstruct a piece or create a remix. Developed with the Chamber Music Society of Detroit and performers including the Aeolus Quartet.

Open restored CMSD Puzzle Cards →

Project context

Browse the earlier general Puzzle Cards restoration for its existing repertoire.

CMSD repertoire

  • School Project — Alexandra T. Bryant, performed by the Aeolus String Quartet. This is the artwork version linked by the original project page.
  • Moanin’ — Bobby Timmons, performed by Marcus Elliot and Michael Malis.

These CMSD sets are designed for Remix: arrange the cards to create your own version of the music. The links open the repertoire directly.

What has been tested

Remix card and audio interactions and original story Submit save/reload were verified at desktop and emulated touch-tablet sizes. Physical iPad and Safari testing remains outstanding.

Availability and limits

New Remix work is saved in this browser through the original story Submit workflow. Local session links do not transfer work to another browser. Historical sessions and Paint remain unavailable.

Credits and provenance

This restoration retains the original interactions and links to existing music and artwork. No recordings are included in this profile.
