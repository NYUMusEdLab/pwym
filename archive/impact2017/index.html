IMPACT2017: a moment in the lab’s history

August 2017

The August 2017 showcase brings together MusEDLab’s tools, collaborations and community, offering a snapshot of its work and reach at that time.

Groove Pizza connects rhythm with shapes and patterns. aQWERTYon opens up melody and harmony through the computer keyboard. Variation Playgrounds, created with the New York Philharmonic and Detroit Symphony Orchestra, invites exploration of how musical ideas can change.

Together, these projects connect playful music making with learning and community collaboration.

The showcase marks the close of the lab’s main New York City development phase, while its research and collaborations continued.

Related presentation / LLK Chat 2017

Designing Technologies & Experiences for Music in the MusEDLab

Presented by S. Alex Ruthmann for the Lifelong Kindergarten Group (MIT Media Lab).

This related LLK presentation explores playful music making and the design of technologies and experiences that lower barriers to musical participation. It connects research, education and design as complementary parts of the MusEDLab’s work.
