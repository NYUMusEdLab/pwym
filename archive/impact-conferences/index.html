Four years of shared experimentation

Explore the conference programs through session titles, credited presenters and surviving abstracts. Keynotes, performances, discussions, demos and hands-on workshops retain their distinct places in the program.

IMPACT stands for Interactive Multimedia Performing Arts Collaborative Technology. These conference records are presented alongside, and separately from, the longer IMPACT workshop described in the historical booklets.

Programs, sources and gaps

The 2014 and 2015 online entries transcribe the printed programs. The 2016 and 2017 entries preserve the public Sched event records. Names and affiliations reflect the historical sources, not current appointments. Missing descriptions are marked in place; registration and lunch entries do not necessarily have abstracts.

Program downloads identify their editions and any archival changes. Original conference artwork is credited where displayed. This collection excludes participant lists, private contact information, proposal reviews, administrative records and confidential planning documents.
