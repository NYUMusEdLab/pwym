Restoration preview: annotations and local camera

Fresh Annotator

A multimedia annotation tool developed for Fresh Ed, connecting hip-hop educational content with drawings, text, audio and video contributions. The restored preview focuses on listening, text and drawing annotations.

Open restored Fresh Annotator →

Try the synthetic demonstration →

What has been tested

Synthetic media playback, text and drawing autosave/reload, independent copies, URL sharing and phone text/share controls. Generated video preview, snapshots, PNG download and camera Stop/Close cleanup were tested live. The published release was compared byte-for-byte with the tested build.

Availability and limits

Historical annotations have not been recovered. Local camera preview and snapshots stay in the tab; download a PNG to keep a snapshot. Images are not attached to saved or shared annotations. No microphone is requested. Recording uploads and CMS metadata logging remain unavailable. Real camera permission grants and physical mobile devices have not been tested. General import/export is not supported. Missing session URLs show an error instead of being silently replaced.

Project context

Fresh Annotator supports culturally responsive learning and student songwriting. The original project date has not been established in the cited website record. The normal launch uses the original Fresh Ed catalog. Original song playback has not been fully tested; the playback checks above used generated tones. The restoration continues to link to existing catalog media; no recordings or private participant data are included here.
