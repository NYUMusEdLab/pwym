Restoration preview: text, drawings and sharing

Fresh Annotator

A multimedia annotation tool developed for Fresh Ed, connecting hip-hop educational content with drawings, text, audio and video contributions. The restored preview focuses on listening, text and drawing annotations.

Try the synthetic demonstration →

What has been tested

Synthetic media playback, text and drawing autosave/reload, independent copies, URL sharing and phone text/share controls. The published release was compared byte-for-byte with the tested build.

Availability and limits

Historical annotations have not been recovered. Recording uploads, camera and CMS metadata logging are unavailable in this release. General import/export is not supported. Missing session URLs show an error instead of being silently replaced.

Project context

Fresh Annotator supports culturally responsive learning and student songwriting. The original project date has not been established in the cited website record. The restoration continues to link to existing catalog media; no recordings or private participant data are included here.
