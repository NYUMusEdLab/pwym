MusEDLab / Collaboration archive

LRNG–ASU

Playlists for learning, creative music making and digital badges.

NYU MusEDLab collaborated with Evan Tobias’s Consortium for Innovation and Transformation in Music Education (CITME) at Arizona State University on digital playlists for music learning. The project was supported by the MacArthur Foundation through the Digital Media & Learning 6 Competition.

The surviving MusEDLab project page and teaching unit connect the partnership with learner-led exploration and the design of online music learning experiences.

From learning playlist to teaching practice

The surviving online music video unit asks music teacher education students to curate, critique, create and evaluate teaching videos. It connects peer discussion and evaluation criteria with making original learning resources.

Design record

The 2017 design collection contains successive graphic guidelines, header artwork and badges. A guideline revision identifies the partnership as NYU MusEDLab and the ASU SMIL lab; the published project page uses CITME. Both descriptions are retained as source context.

The badge shown here is the “Building Instruments & Interfaces” design. Other header candidates include third-party photographs whose reuse terms have not been established, so they are not reproduced.

Archive coverage

This page preserves the collaboration’s context and a verified original graphic. The full playlist curriculum, badge-issuing service and current status of the external LRNG platform have not been restored. Dates of every playlist release and individual artwork authorship remain unverified.
