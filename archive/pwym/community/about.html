ABOUT THIS EDITION

Play With Your Music
Community archive

A selected, read-only edition of discussions and course material from 2014–2020. Accounts, posting, private messages and original interactive media services are not restored.

Source and coverage

This edition is derived from the preserved November 7, 2024 forum snapshot. The project owner confirmed that there was no additional content after March 2020. It is an edited public reading copy, not a complete reproduction of the private preservation archive.

Selection and privacy

176 conversation records contain 1,497 readable ordinary posts. 881 posts are replaced by privacy notices. Private-message and restricted-category content, account profiles and contact records are excluded. Replies in the two main introduction threads, the school-age group and flagged personal or sensitive passages are withheld. Participant handles are replaced by stable labels; references to public artists and project staff may remain. Forty-nine nonordinary post entries and three unrelated spam topics are excluded.

The historical site-wide login setting is absent from the preservation derivative. This edition does not claim that every included discussion was originally available without an account.

Images, links and missing material

The edition includes 47 selected course illustrations and music-analysis images. Identifying participant images, uncertain images, original audio/video, external embeds and unavailable attachments remain omitted. Existing external links are withheld. Omissions are labelled; they are not replaced with invented content. Original private files remain unchanged.

Course order and team contributions

The five sections follow the preserved Play With Your Audio curriculum and the forum categories: Getting Started, Sounds and Space, Musical Structure, Music Multitracks and Mix Balancing. They describe the later preserved course, rather than every week of the original 2013 pilot.

PWYM Team labels identify Alex Ruthmann, Ethan Hein and Dirk Uys from creator credits and archived account metadata. Alex Case is labelled separately as a course contributor. Participant labels and the existing public selection are retained.

Reading this archive

Browse five curriculum sections and a separate community collection. Search and six-item pagination work locally in your browser. Topic and post numbers preserve references where possible. A link to an excluded post may lead to its topic or an omission notice. Nothing you type into archive search is submitted to a forum server.
