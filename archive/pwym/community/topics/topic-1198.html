A group for people interested in using, or currently using, PG Music's Band-In-A Box or RealBand in their music making process.

I use the 2014 UltraPlusPak edition of Band-In-A-Box and RealBandto to create backing tracks and practice audio files. I also use Cakewalk Music Creator 6 software digital audio workstation (DAW), Audacity wave editor, Zoom MRS-8 8 track hardware digital workstation, acoustic and electric guitar and a keyboard. Not that great with any of it but enjoy it all.

My primary music interest is in country, rock and easy listening music from the late fifities to early 2000.
