Play With Your Audio (#PWYA) is the new home of the original Play With Your Music course content on the creative processes of audio producers and engineers featuring the music of Clara Berry [link withheld] and Peter Gabriel [link withheld].

We're expanding the content here at Play With Your Music and we're consolidating the prior course here as Play With Your Audio!

We've also shared the current course module content on Github and made it available for you to download, fork, remix, and submit pull requests to update and add new content to #PWYM and Play With Your Audio.
