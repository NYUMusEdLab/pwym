PWYM TeamEthan Hein2014-05-25#2

Study the song structures of music you like, and emulate them. Once you have a bunch of sections together -- hook, breakdown etc -- then try organizing them according to the structure of a tune you like. Find the equivalent between your sections and the ones in the tune, to the verse, chorus, bridge and so on. Here are some examples to get you started. [external link withheld] [link withheld]
