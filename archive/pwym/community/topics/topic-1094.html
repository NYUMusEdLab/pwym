Hi all,
Just discovered a great app for iOS called CAPO. I've been wanting to learn the Neil Young harmonica part in the song "Out on the Weekend" but struggled because I couldn't slow the song down to hear/find/play the distinct notes. Tried the app CAPO and it slows the song down at different settings (1/4, 1/2, 3/4) so I was able to hear/find/play each and every harmonica note. What a gem for trying to learn difficult parts of songs!
