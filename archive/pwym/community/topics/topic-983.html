If you have a small budget I'd recommend the Volca Beats! It's 130$ and has 6 pure analog voices (kick, snare, oh, CH, high tom and low tom) and 2 lo-fi sample based voices (clap and agogo)... the kick is really deep. The snare sounds kind of bad but it's easily fixable with a small mod.
If you can afford more and you want analog check out the Elektron Rytm it's a beast! Mr. Dataline on YouTube did several cool demos of it's full potential!

Well the monotribe is indeed more polyvalent but you only get 3 voices of un-editable (unless you're comfortable with modding) drums and a 1osc monosynth.
For the Volca series, I think it's more adapted for people that want more in a specific category (drum machine/bass/lead): they are less polyvalent but really more tweakable in what they do.
There is also the fact that they have MIDI-in and a tactile keyboard instead of a ribbon controller.
But if you want polyvalence against completude (??), and especially if you're okay with DIY modding, the monotribe is a great choice IMO...

Embedded media omitted from this public edition

The attached Space Graph is here:
TOTCSpaceGraph.pdf [link withheld] (88.4 KB)

I have divided the song into four sections based on the expression of different melodic themes. The Space Graph is a static representation of the second section beginning around 3:49 (just after Chris Squire's "I am sure we know" background vocal) and extends via an instrumental build-up to the crashing cymbal at ~5:17.

I particularly like this song for several reasons:
tasteful virtuosic playing without any excess by the guitarist, pianist and drummer:
the drummer does not lay down conventional drum rhythms but instead contributes melodically using tuned percussion and cymbals;
the arrangement is distinctly original and the emotional power conveyed by the song is reflected by the layers of vibraphone harmonics and Polymoog pads drenched in reverb.

In addition to the deep emotions elicited by this music, the song is also particularly challenging to dissect technically. For these reasons, I find it irresistible.

I hope that you like it too.
