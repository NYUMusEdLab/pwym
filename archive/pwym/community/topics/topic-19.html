Clara Berry & Wooldog - Air Traffic

1 readable posts · Team contributions are labelled separately from community replies.

PWYM TeamAlex Ruthmann2014-04-15#1

Image omitted from this public edition. Air Traffic: Listen on Soundcloud [link withheld]

Clara Berry grew up by the sea in Southern Maine, writing delicate, twisting melodies and enchanting story-based lyrics. Meanwhile Joe O'Neill, the primary force of Wooldog, grew up on Boston's South Shore garnering influence from Billy Martin and John Zorn whilst wailing on pickle buckets in the Cambridge streets. The two met at a show in Lowell, MA and later found their styles to be perfectly complimentary.

Not long after they would commence recording with producer Bradford Swanson [link withheld] (The Bad Plus, Mason Jennings) on a full-length album released in October of 2013. The Wire NH & The Portland Press Herald named it among the best local records of 2013. The first track off the album, "Air Traffic [link withheld]" was used in the first free online music production course [link withheld] presented by NYU Steinhardt [link withheld], MIT Participant 751 Lab and P2PU [link withheld].

Their music has been featured on The Bob Edwards Show, Soundcheck, The River Boston and a number of local stations throughout New England. In December of 2013 the duo recorded and released the single "Riches & The Girls [link withheld]" with Dean Baltulonis (The Hold Steady, Gay Blades) a preview of things to come in 2014.
