David Gates and / or Bread. David Gates Biography [link withheld] David Gates was a successful rock singer, songwriter and producer throughout the sixties and seventies. His music with Bread features the highest production values with liberal use of double tracking, self harmony, efficient use of effects to create a tonal space and great use of equalization to allow each instrument it's own space.

Keb Mo'. Keb Mo' Biography [link withheld] His music is current and contemporary with much of his past work recorded in house.

Marty Stuart. Marty Stuart Biography [link withheld] Marty Stuart is a successful pop. country and bluegrass singer, songwriter and producer. He is also a traditionalist with a keen ear for making what's old sound relevant in today's world.
