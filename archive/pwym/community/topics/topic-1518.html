580Participant 5802014-06-29#19

"Alive" - as recorded by "Empire Of The Sun"

Embedded media omitted from this public edition

The structure of "Alive" breaks from the rule for section lengths in multiples of 4. Most sections are 8 measures plus 2. The first three sections of verse and chorus also add a 1 measure breakdown, for a total of 11 measures in the section.
The song uses different voices for verse, chorus and break. Everything drops out for verse 3 except for some closed hi hat and synth. Some instruments join for the second half of verse 3. All the sounds return for the final chorus.

This song has an unusual structure when taken a closer look. It's outro/intro sounds like a reprise of Breath track, which is the second song in the album from Dark Side of the Moon.This makes the song sound like two different songs in one track which is quite engaging. The third chorus of Time although changes the mood from the original chorus does still manage to give the impression of familiarity of the original chorus but still sounding slightly out of place. I also really like how the organ is used a lot during the chorus, gives quite the feeling of peeking like opening up.

Link to the Google Doc is here [link withheld]. For an ostensibly 'progressive' song, the basic structure of Frame by Frame is actually quite regular, and bears some resemblance to a lot of jazz music in having a predictable, consisting of the statement of a theme, followed by developments of that theme, then moving into another theme(s), before returning to recapitulate that theme later on and moving through the structure again. Most of the complexity lies in the individual parts, modulation and varying tonality, polyrhythm, and the general use of complex meter.
