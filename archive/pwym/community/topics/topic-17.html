Tips: How and where to participate in PWYM 2.0

15 readable posts · Team contributions are labelled separately from community replies.

PWYM TeamAlex Ruthmann2014-04-14#1

Sign up for email updates on the PWYM homepage [link withheld].

You can participate in a variety of ways:

  • Discussion forums: That's here, in the PWYM Community. Click Community [link withheld] to get to the main list of discussions.
  • Live sessions: Also known as "Unhangouts." You'll be able to participate in live chats alongside videos from the PWYM unhangout [link withheld].
  • Videos: We'll post videos and interviews within each Module for the course, and on the PWYM YouTube channel.
  • Activities: Each week there will be an activity and an opportunity to play with your music and work directly with the music of Peter Gabriel.
  • Twitter: The hashtag you can use is: #pwym [link withheld]. Follow us
    [participant mention] [link withheld].
  • Soundcloud: Share and discuss your music over in our PWYM Soundcloud Group [link withheld].
  • Locally: Look in Meet [link withheld] (and Map [link withheld]) to see if you can find others in your area. Or invite friends, family, colleagues to try the activities with you.

Not enough? If you're looking for other ways to engage...

  • Virtual Groups: You can also invite or find out about times to discuss or hangout with others in the Meet forum [link withheld]. Feel free to organize yourself into groups as you get to know each other.
  • Previous PWYM G+: Some people are still posting in the previous G+ community space [link withheld] (though we don't have time to weed through the spam, you can still join).

For now, it will be a manual process of keeping GitHub and PWYM in sync.
