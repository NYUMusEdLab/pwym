Coursera is a MOOC and its by far the most popular and biggest with a very large number of colleges on board. I have completed a number of class on Coursera. I just finished developing your musicianship and currently I am taking History of Rock n Rock part 1 and Songwriting.

Also Coursera is better set up than any other MOOC's I have tried, I have tried out at least 4 or 5 others(and now this one) and Coursera just seems so much more professional and well throught out, all the others I just ended up dropping out of. But on Coursera I have finished 5 or 6 classes.

Edit: I should point Coursera is the best for deadline based courses, Khan's Academy(for math) and Udacity are both very good for self paced free online courses.
