Community replyParticipant 5012014-05-25#48

Basically, all jazz, R&B, hip hop, some pop and rock "leans". It's a way to count music so that you can find the nuances of the beat. I just used lean as an example to translate cause there's songs say lean in them and stuff. So, just stand up and count 1&2&3&4& and on the & lean slightly to one side (or bend one knee slightly) and repeat it. Then apply it to a piece of music in 4/4 time. I think it's necessary for a song to mix up for the dance floor lol. Let me know if that makes sense to you or not:)

A shuffle is a feel written in 4/4 and counted as 12/8. There are slight variations on it, as in, Kansas City Shuffle, Blues shuffle, Country Honky Tonk, etc. 1/8 rock and 1/16 rock are also common feels. The guys that play with me, they will just say, blues shuffle in E or something and take off playing. I thought that is what Wikipedia meant when it said it gave you choices in playing style; because, that's what I know that to mean. I was just trying to move us into something that had a downbeat. It will be more friendly to work with, as contemporary musicians with different approaches to theory. Since the piano players probably read really well and the guitar players may or may not at all.
