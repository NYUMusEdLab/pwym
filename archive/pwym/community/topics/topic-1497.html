Robert Plant's echo in Led Zeppelin's 'Whole Lotta Love' was an accident. Who knew? [link withheld]

Guitarist Jimmy Page, recording engineer George Chkiantz and final-mix engineer Eddie Kramer on vocalist Robert Plant's vocals and everything else behind the making of the 1968 classic "Whole Lotta Love"

One of the tastiest details is that the famous pre-echo on Robert Plant's voice was the result of undesired crosstalk on the mixing desk. There was no way to get rid of it, so they ended up just throwing some reverb on it and making into part of the track. Treasure those studio accidents!
