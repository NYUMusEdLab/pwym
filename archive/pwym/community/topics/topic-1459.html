Updates on Play With Your Music course sign up and community engagement

1 readable posts · Team contributions are labelled separately from community replies.

PWYM TeamAlex Ruthmann2014-05-30#1

Hi all... We're an open education project and I thought some of you may be interested in updates about our Play With Your Music course and community. Here's a quick quantitative snapshot for the first two weeks of PWYM 2.0:

Data Snapshot (May 30):

  • 21,125 unique visitors to our course website - [external link withheld] [link withheld]
  • 7,741 people signed up for the course = 37% of website visitors signed up for the course
  • 5,583 unique users are consuming content in the PWYM Community - [external link withheld] [link withheld].
  • 1,306 are active, posting members of the PWYM Community
  • 1,033 people have placed themselves on the PWYM world map: [external link withheld] [link withheld]
  • 62 user created small working groups were created by participants in the Meet category of the PWYM Community.
  • An average visit to the PWYM Community site is 6.36 minutes long with a 3% bounce rate (a "bounce" is counted when visitors view the main page and then leave the site without following an additional link)
