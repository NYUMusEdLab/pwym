PWYM TeamAlex Ruthmann2014-06-13#5

[participant mention] [link withheld] I'll ask Phil and see if he'll respond here. In my interactions with Phil, I've taken away that good songwriting is about that creative balance between expectation and surprise. Chord progressions and melodic shapes can be manipulated to reinforce expectation or induce surprise.... and, so can lyrics, and that extends to meter and even song forms. One of my favorite examples is the Beatles' Yesterday, which has 7 bar phrases ... one less than you'd normally expect. But, it sure does a good job of setting up the first lyric of the second verse - "suddenly." That, coupled with the entrance of the string parts really makes an impact, I think. To me that's a good example where the recording/production techniques reinforce the song.
