Play With Your Music 2.0 - Peter Gabriel Edition will start on May 16, 2014! PWYM 2.0 will unfold over 10 weeks including interactive listening and production assignments, discussions and interviews. All of this will be augmented by live and pre-recorded interviews with musicians and collaborators who've worked closely with Peter Gabriel over his long career.

PWYM 2.0 will follow the same general trajectory of projects as PWYM 1.0 - critical listening, recording analysis, audio FX, mixing, and audio and MIDI remixing - but through interactive experiences directly exploring the multitrack recordings of Peter Gabriel's hits Sledgehammer and In Your Eyes. We invite you to apply the ideas in this course to your own music making and learning experiences. Together, we can reimagine and reinvent audio education.

Play With Your Music continues to be a collaborative experiment for us. We plan to continue to tinker with the course format and explore new tools for sharing ideas and supporting your music making, learning and education. We hope you will tinker with us.
