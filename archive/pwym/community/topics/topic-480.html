I'm a beginner so I found the perceived space graph to be difficult but quite interesting. I've attempted to graph the section of the song from about 1:17 to 2:40. I've identified the following timbres, in order of their appearance (in the graph, the less prominent sounds are shown with a lighter border):

Lead/Background Vocals: Strong female voice with thick reverb (and BG vocals to far left/right)
Piano: Normal piano sound, melodic, with some notes scattered to far left or right
Kick:Heavy "thump" bass drum
Whoosh: Processed transition effect of wind/surf
Synth: Slightly distorted electronic chords, multiple layers, replaces piano later in track
Drum Machine: Digital snare, typical drum machine sound
