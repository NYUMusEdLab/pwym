PWYM TeamEthan Hein2014-05-27#30

Web-based audio production is still in its infancy, so none of the online tools are as robust as their offline equivalents. If you've used other DAWs, you're going to find Soundation pretty primitive. My attitude is, it's a miracle that such things are possible at all, and as the HTML 5 standard deploys further with its very sophisticated audio capabilities, there's going to be a rapid advance in web-based audio tools. It's sort of like the early days of desktop DAWs -- things were awkward and limited at first, but evolution was swift.
