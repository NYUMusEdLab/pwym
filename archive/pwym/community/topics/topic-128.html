Can we use our own DAW systems for the course, or do we have to use the online suite?

PWYM TeamAlex Ruthmann2014-05-14#10

Hi,

Our license with Mr. Gabriel allows us to make the multitrack stems available to you through our custom web-based mixing interfaces and via custom Soundcloud.com sites. We are not allowed to make the stems available to you outside our tools.

This course is equally about you working with your own music, Peter's music, or the music of others, so many of the projects you can choose to work on using your own DAW, just not if you want to work directly with Peter's mulitrack stems.
