MusEDLab / Community archive

Ed Sullivan Fellows → CORE Music

One evolving community of young artists, mentors and collaborative music makers.

This combined archive follows the Ed Sullivan Fellows program and its successor, CORE Music. Led by Jamie Ehrenfeld within the NYU Music Experience Design Lab, the work centered on artist mentorship, songwriting, recording, performance and creative community.

The names describe successive phases of the same program history. This page keeps those histories together while allowing each period’s published sources to speak in its own terms.

Ed Sullivan Fellows

Ethan Hein’s 2017 account describes an artist development and mentorship program meeting at NYU Steinhardt. Fellows wrote and recorded music, explored the music business and branding, and learned through informal collaboration with musicians and mentors.

The 2016 IMPACT program includes an opening showcase featuring the Fellows and a workshop led by Jamie Ehrenfeld. The 2017 conference’s MusEDLab showcase also identifies Fellows performances.

CORE Music

By March 2018, the public record uses the CORE Music name. Hein’s account of a March 3 cypher describes a mentorship lab, event series and professional network, with participants writing, recording, rehearsing, listening and studying together. It identifies Jamie Ehrenfeld as the program leader and says the participants chose the CORE name.

The exact changeover date has not been established in the reviewed public sources. CORE is retained as a name, without inventing an expansion of its letters.
