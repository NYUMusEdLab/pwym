Play the space

  1. Choose one of the six original scenes.
  2. Click a green parameter on the left and bottom for music. Blue parameters on the top and right control video.
  3. Drag Drums, Lead, Bass, or Chords. Moving right or up increases the selected values.
  4. Record a take, then use Replay or Visualize to review it.

Original Escape shortcuts

Hold Escape while clicking Record for clean video; Video for camera; an empty area for handles; a border for control visibility; Share for networking. AV adds Escape+Replay for snapshots and Escape+Play for reset. JamSkolan’s native quit shortcut returns to the scene lobby here.

Use the instrument buttons and sliders for keyboard access. Arrow keys move the selected handle; Shift gives smaller steps. Space toggles playback.

 Research & teaching reconstruction

Original editions. Recovered materials.

This browser edition is grounded in the recovered Jam2Jam AV 1.1c, standard Jam2JamSkolan, and Jam2jam AV Visualize application bundles. It uses their scene MIDI and interface artwork, with samples decoded from archived soundfonts.

The musical versions retain different pitch and articulation rules. Visualize retains the original two-value filtering and activity histograms. Browser takes additionally preserve actual generated notes and separate reflective decisions.

Browser adaptations

  • Web Audio replaces the original Impromptu / Apple Audio Unit renderer; synthesis envelopes, filters, and video effects are approximations.
  • Missing General MIDI patches use an explicitly reported archived-bank substitute.
  • Shared controls use a prepared WebSocket service, with same-browser rooms available locally. Audio, video, scene selection and playback remain local. The hosted service has not yet been deployed or tested across devices.
  • Recordings use supported browser media formats, are stored on this device, and are never automatically deleted.
  • Original native applications have not been run for an audible or pixel-by-pixel equivalence test.

Play together

This public preview has no online room service or suite sign-in. Use solo mode or tabs in this browser. On a separately configured host with a room service, signing in can connect you automatically. Select an instrument and move its X/Y controls or sliders: the changed parameter values reach everyone in your room. Everyone can move any control.

Sound, images, camera, microphone, scene selection, playback and recordings stay in each browser. Selecting a different axis changes only your own controls.
