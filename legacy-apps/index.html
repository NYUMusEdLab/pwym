Research recreation · public preview

Jam2Jam

Explore Jam2Jam AV, JamSkolan and Visualize, including recovered scenes, samples and Sousa settings. Shape musical parameters, capture a take and revisit the performance.

Browser reconstruction, not the original native software. Solo and same-browser rooms are available; hosted multi-user rooms and suite sign-in are not enabled. Keep exported takes before clearing browser storage.

XPress interface study · v0.4

Super Duper Music Looper

Paint and erase loops, adjust key and tempo, and explore LooperVision using recovered XPress factory sounds and artwork.

A browser study of Sonic Foundry/Sony XPress 2.0. Timing and pitch processing are approximations; native SDML saving and audio export are not implemented. Original product names and assets remain credited to their owners.

Public browser recreation

Melody Morph

Arrange sounding objects, draw musical paths and explore a landscape of melodies. Based on Eric Rosenbaum’s original MelodyMorph concept and design.

Opens the existing workshop release. Save and export work locally; central terrain catalog and account services are not enabled.
