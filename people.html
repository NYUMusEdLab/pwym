Dr. Ethan Hein

Music Adjunct Faculty, NYU; adjunct professor, The New School

Permanent members

Biography & links for Dr. Ethan Hein

Founding MusEDLab member and lead designer of Groove Pizza, bringing music education and creative technology into dialogue.

Ethan holds a PhD in music education from NYU, where he teaches music theory, music history and songwriting. His work with the lab includes Groove Pizza and aQWERTYon, tools that invite learners to explore rhythm, harmony and musical expression.

With Will Kuhn, he coauthored Electronic Music School: A Contemporary Approach to Teaching Musical Creativity (Oxford University Press, 2021). He shares his teaching and musical ideas through his blog, podcast and MusicRadar column.

Joshua Emanuel

Music educator · A. MacArthur Barr Middle School, Nanuet

At MusEDLab: Music education researcher; NYU Music Education PhD, 2026.

Biography & links for Joshua Emanuel

Research on songwriting and classroom music learning.

Joshua is a music educator, percussionist and songwriter.

PhD in Music Education, NYU Steinhardt, 2026

A Design-Based, Practitioner Inquiry into Developing a Middle School Songwriting Curriculum

Joshua’s doctoral research examines how songwriting teaching develops through the iterative design of an eighth-grade music curriculum. Combining practitioner inquiry, his own songwriting and dialogue with fellow educators, it explores curricular structure and creative freedom, constructive feedback and teacher identity. Findings offer practical principles for songwriting instruction and professional learning communities.

MT

Meghan Todt Williams

Violinist, educator and artistic producer · New York / NYU teaching

At MusEDLab: Music education researcher; NYU Music Education PhD, 2026.

Biography & links for Meghan Todt Williams

Research associate with the NYU NEA Research Lab.

Meghan performs as a violinist and teaches violin, chamber music and pedagogy. She earned a BA at Princeton and an MM at NYU; her work combines performance, teaching and arts research.

PhD in Music Education, NYU Steinhardt, 2026

“This Is Who I Am”: Mapping the Education, Careers, and Lives of Interdisciplinary Musicians in the United States

Meghan’s doctoral research examines how musicians combine intensive musical training with other fields of study to build their careers and lives. A questionnaire with 98 respondents and two interview rounds with 10 interdisciplinary musicians informed narrative and voice-centered analysis of nonlinear, evolving careers. It offers guidance for young musicians considering interdisciplinary higher education and connections between musical study, work and lifelong music making.

NYU profile Personal website
