Jam2Jam

Explore Jam2Jam AV, JamSkolan and Visualize, including recovered scenes, samples and Sousa settings. Shape musical parameters, capture a take and revisit the performance.

Browser reconstruction, not the original native software. Solo and same-browser rooms are available; hosted multi-user rooms and suite sign-in are not enabled. Keep exported takes before clearing browser storage.

Research & reading

  • Dillon, S. (2006). Jam2jam: Networked Jamming. M/C Journal, 9(6). Networked improvisation and collaborative musical engagement.
  • Brown, A. R., Dillon, S., Kerr, T., & Sorensen, A. (2009). Evolving interactions: Agile design for networked media performance. OzCHI ’09, 41–48. Interaction design across the development of Jam2Jam.

Super Duper Music Looper

Paint and erase loops, adjust key and tempo, and explore LooperVision using recovered XPress factory sounds and artwork.

A browser study of Sonic Foundry/Sony XPress 2.0. Timing and pitch processing are approximations; native SDML saving and audio export are not implemented. Original product names and assets remain credited to their owners.

Research & reading

  • Heyworth, J. (2011). Jumping through ‘loops’: A reflective study on preparing generalist pre-service teachers to teach music. Issues in Educational Research, 21(1), 42–64. Discusses Super Duper Music Looper in teacher preparation, including its timeline and classroom activities.

Melody Morph

Arrange sounding objects, draw musical paths and explore a landscape of melodies. Based on Eric Rosenbaum’s original MelodyMorph concept and design.

Opens the existing workshop release. Save and export work locally; central terrain catalog and account services are not enabled.

Research & reading

  • Rosenbaum, E. (2011). MelodyMorph: A Reconfigurable Musical Instrument (PDF). Proceedings of NIME 2011, 445–447. The original design paper on sounding bells, recorder bells and reconfigurable melodic landscapes.

Continuator

Play a musical phrase and hear a response learned from your own material. This new browser interface runs François Pachet’s official Continuator 2.0 engine.

Modern engine adaptation, not a facsimile of the historical interface. First load downloads browser Python. When a phrase cannot continue from its final note, the engine explicitly starts a new phrase from learned memory. Notes stay in this tab. No microphone or account required.

Research & teaching

  • Pachet (2003), The Continuator: Musical Interaction With Style
  • Addessi & Pachet (2005), Experiments with a musical machine

More publications, original demonstrations and new listening prompts are included in the study.

MIROR · Impro / Compo

Explore musical dialogue and composition from the learner’s own musical memory. This browser study follows documented MIROR-Impro and MIROR-Compo workflows using the updated Continuator engine.

Partial interaction reconstruction, not the original MIROR software or a validated research instrument. MIROR-Body is documented but not replicated. First load downloads browser Python; export sessions locally before closing.

Documentation & research

  • Khatchatourov & Pachet (2013), MIROR Impro and Compo user guide
  • Addessi (2020), MIROR platform and learning overview
