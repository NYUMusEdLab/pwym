Designing for musical participation

The NYU Music Experience Design Lab (MusEDLab) is a globally recognized research, design, and learning innovation studio that develops creative technologies and experiences to support music, arts, and cultural learning, creativity, and engagement across the lifespan. Based at NYU Shanghai, the Lab collaborates with educators, students, artists, nonprofits, and industry partners around the world. Its work spans creative app development, immersive educational tools, participatory design, and curriculum innovation, with a focus on accessibility and inclusivity. Through widely used tools like Groove Pizza, Variation Playgrounds, and Play With Your Music, the MusEDLab has reached millions of learners and educators globally, supporting communities in the U.S. & Canada, Central America, Europe, and Asia through culturally responsive and research-driven initiatives.

Our history

The MusEDLab also serves as a creative incubator for student projects across NYU's Global Network of campuses. The MusEDLab was founded in September 2013 by S. Alex Ruthmann. Since 2019, the Lab is housed within the NYU Shanghai Program on Creativity and Innovation.
