Preserved learning toolkit

MathScienceMusic

Math, Science & Music is a free toolkit for teachers presenting engaging curricula, games, apps, and other online elements that integrate music, math, and science into teaching and learning materials for K-12 and college students. Launched on International Jazz Day in 2016, the Thelonious Monk Institute of Jazz, together with jazz legend Herbie Hancock, collaborated with international math, science, and music experts at seven leading universities to develop the program.
