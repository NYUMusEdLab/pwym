Explore an authorized copy in this browser

  1. Obtain the selected province ZIP and matching curated JSONL file(s) from the official links. Compare archive SHA256 against the upstream manifest.
  2. Download the local Python importer. It makes no network calls and does not upload files.
  3. Run with Python3.9 or later. For Shanghai:
python3 prepare-anthology.py --archive ACFS-v261103-scores-shanghai.zip --metadata shanghai.jsonl --output shanghai-local.json

For Shaanxi, provide both volume metadata files:

python3 prepare-anthology.py --archive YOUR-SHAANXI-ARCHIVE.zip --metadata shaanxi1.jsonl --metadata shaanxi2.jsonl --output shaanxi-local.json

In the explorer, choose Sources / help → Local index file. Select the generated JSON, then select its collection. Search contours, see indexed notes and lyrics, and hear a synthesized rendering. Reloading clears imported data. This does not grant rights to republish the index.

Limitations: first-part sequential melody only; chord tones and grace notes omitted, repeats not unfolded. Jianpu uses1=C as a display reference. Numeric tempo candidates preserve their original beat units and are explicitly unreviewed; unknown or missing units use a labeled preview fallback. No tempo is inferred from descriptive words.
