Sources, reuse and privacy

This non-commercial learning interface includes389 licensed Bach soprano lines,24 Winterreise vocal lines and12 ViolinI melodic reductions from Mozart symphonies39–41. It does not include all Schubert Lieder, Mozart concerti or complete orchestral playback.

Data adaptations are provided under CC BY-NC-SA4.0: credit the original sources and this adaptation, identify changes, use non-commercially and share adapted data under the same licence. Complete attribution and changes.

Bach: Alberda / Hentschel389 settings · Chorale Corpus; soprano only, not full harmony or recordings.

Schubert: DCMLab Winterreise. Mozart: Digital Interactive Mozart Edition / Neue Mozart-Ausgabe, Internationale Stiftung Mozarteum Salzburg. Neither institution endorses this adaptation.

Shanghai and the earlier music21 Bach encodings are not redistributed because their applicable reuse permissions have not been verified. The included Bach data comes instead from the separately licensed Alberda/Hentschel389-setting edition, using TSV extraction rather than error-prone MusicXML conversions. Sources: Chinese Folk Song Anthology and music21 chorales. You may load your own authorized derived-index JSON locally below. Raw MusicXML/MEI import is not implemented.

Local files are read in browser memory, never uploaded by this app.

Privacy: no app analytics, cookies, accounts, research logging or server upload. Your drawings, searches and local files stay in browser memory and clear on reload. The static hosting provider may retain ordinary web-request logs; source links open external sites with their own policies.

Notation cards and piano roll reconstruct the selected source line, not a full engraved score. Audio is synthesized, not a performance. Repeats are not unfolded; grace notes are omitted; polyphony is reduced to the selected voice/top chord tone. Numeric playback tempos can be editorial defaults. Approximate contour search ignores rhythm; its DTW distance is not confidence or cultural similarity. Lyrics stay as encoded. Technical limitations.
