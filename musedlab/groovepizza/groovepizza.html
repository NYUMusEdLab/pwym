Groove Pizza

Groove Pizza is a circular rhythm app for creative music making and learning! It connects music creation with math concepts like shapes, angles, and patterns. Debuted at the U.S. Department of Education in 2016, it has received acclaim from the Washington Post, USA Today, and Julian Lennon.

Use pizza "specials," shapes, and toppings to craft grooves. Export as audio or MIDI, or share to Soundtrap, Noteflight, or Flat.io. Explore more via the Flat.io blog.

History & Credits

Inspired by Ethan Hein's NYU thesis, Groove Pizza was co-developed with Adam November. Original development began in 2015, launching with support from MathScienceMusic.org in 2016.
