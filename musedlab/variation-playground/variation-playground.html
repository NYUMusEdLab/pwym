Variation Playgrounds

The New York Philharmonic commissioned us to design musical variation play platforms for their Young People's Concerts Play! initiative. These interactive playgrounds help children creatively explore variation in classical repertoire.

Available Variation Playgrounds include:

  • Benjamin Britten – Young Person's Guide to the Orchestra
  • Felix Mendelssohn – Midsummer Night's Dream
  • Antonin Dvořák – New World Symphony
  • Jean Sibelius – Tone Poems
  • Harlem Renaissance Playground

The Detroit Symphony Orchestra also commissioned a Variation Playground for their DSO Classroom children's concert Happy, Sad, Silly, Scary.
