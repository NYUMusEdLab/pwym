The Movement NotebookProject & collaborators

Keep the movement

Move individual sound sources to explore the spatial mix. The Movement Notebook lets you capture, save and replay movement takes, returning to the paths you create through sound.

Project & collaborators

Yiting Liu created the completed prototype. MusEDLab has further developed the current version with a green interface and a Movement Notebook for capturing, saving and replaying movement takes.

Spatial Music Lab opens with Chinese Instrumental, featuring music by Justin Scholar 玉刻.

The project connects with MusEDLab’s collaboration with Yiting Liu on traditional instrumental performances, immersive multichannel recording, spatial listening and interactive media.
