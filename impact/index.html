Artistic and design inquiry

Listening to learn

From MusicDelta and Step Inside Mahler to Aeolus recordings and Boulez’s spatial music, these projects ask what listeners can discover by acting on the parts and perspectives inside a recording.

Published research

Learning to theorise music

The Notio collaboration connects songwriting, improvisation and music theory in Finland. Publications examine research methods and emergent lesson design, making the inquiry available beyond the tool itself.

04

Research collaboration

Sustainable careers in music

The NEA Research Lab on Sustainable Entrepreneurship in the Performing Arts brings together Alex Ruthmann, Bruce Carter and Tanya Kalmanovitch. Its research agenda includes entrepreneurial practice in classical music training and the New World Symphony BLUE program.

Current design questions

Rengongfu explores musical judgment, practice and visible learning processes. Its concepts and prototypes are in development. The project page presents design intentions and questions for research, without claiming established learning effects.
