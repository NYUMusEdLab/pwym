Make your listening visible

Choose a recording, focus on a section, then name and place the sounds you hear. Left and right describe stereo position. Near and far describe perceived distance. Width describes stereo spread.

Your interpretation matters

There are no automatically detected instruments or correct positions here. Moving or widening a mark does not isolate, pan, or change a sound in the recording. Use tentative labels and notes when you are unsure.

See and edit sections

The section timeline shows named passages against recording time. Select a block to cue its map. Drag the selected start/end edges, use their arrow keys (Shift for one-second steps), or type exact times above the timeline. Escape cancels a drag. Focus section magnifies a short passage; Show all restores the whole recording. Overlapping readings appear in separate rows; scroll within the strip to see more. Click the time ruler or use the recording-position slider to seek. Split at playhead divides the selected section and copies its map into the new section for further editing. Edit details opens naming, boundary and delete controls.

Listen, then compare

Set section boundaries in Details → Section or use the playhead buttons. Duplicate a map to follow the same sounds into another section; then change its times and positions. Compare shows both maps with the same coordinate system. A sound can be removed from one section when it is absent.

Files and privacy

Audio is decoded in memory on this device. No upload, account, cloud storage, or analytics collection is used. Maps autosave in this browser. Export JSON for a portable backup: it includes your notes and recording filename, size, duration, and SHA-256 identity, but never audio. Private browsing, clearing site data, or changing browser/address can remove local maps.

After reload or import, choose the exact same audio file to relink it. The fingerprint prevents attaching a different recording to your timestamps. For a different recording, start a new project. Keep your original audio separately; share recordings only when you have permission.

Keyboard and touch

Tab to a sound; arrow keys move it (Shift for a larger step). Details provides labeled sliders and a sound selector as alternatives to dragging. Space plays or pauses when focus is outside an editing control. In Draw mode, focus the stage: arrows move the cursor, Enter adds a point, and Finish trace saves it. Escape cancels an unfinished trace. Pointer drawing and Undo trace are also available.

Where this comes from
