2016 · Archival listening prototype

Four performances.
New perspectives.

Alex Ruthmann and the NYU Music Experience Design Lab developed Step Inside Mahler with the New York Philharmonic and Norwegian developer MusIT, using MusIT’s OIID technology.

The prototype brought together four historical performances of Mahler’s Sixth Symphony, conducted by Dimitri Mitropoulos, Leonard Bernstein, Lorin Maazel and Alan Gilbert, with annotated scores from the Philharmonic’s Leon Levy Digital Archives.

Its score interface invited listeners to compare performances alongside the archival scores. A companion spatial-listening interface offered different instrumental perspectives within the orchestra.

10 February 2016

Stepping Inside Mahler’s Sixth

The public event took place at 7:30 p.m. at the David Rubenstein Atrium at Lincoln Center, as part of Insights at the Atrium, co-presented with Lincoln Center for the Performing Arts.

Interactive iPad stations let visitors explore the prototype. The event brought together Alex Ruthmann, historian Erik Ryding, Philharmonic Audio Director Lawrence Rock, and archivist and historian Barbara Haws.

Project partners: NYU MusEDLab, the New York Philharmonic and MusIT. Technology: OIID. Archival materials: the New York Philharmonic Leon Levy Digital Archives. Music and archival rights remain with their respective rights holders.
