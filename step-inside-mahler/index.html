Step Inside Mahler

Step Inside Mahler’s Sixth is a public event and interactive prototype developed by Prof. Alex Ruthmann and the NYU MusEDLab in collaboration with MusIT using OIID technology. It enables exploration of Mahler’s Sixth Symphony through multiple historical recordings and annotated scores.

The app includes performances by Dimitri Mitropoulos, Leonard Bernstein, Lorin Maazel, and Alan Gilbert, along with historic scores from the NY Philharmonic Leon Levy Digital Archives.
