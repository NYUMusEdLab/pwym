Creativity and Innovation Program

Creative Learning Design – Summer 2019

Location: Shanghai, China
Credits: 4
Instructor: S. Alex Ruthmann

This hands-on course explores the design of engaging creative learning experiences using music, technology, and user-centered design. Students prototype digital learning tools for Chinese youth in collaboration with schools and cultural partners in Shanghai.

Course Structure

  • Class discussions & presentations
  • Group projects and readings
  • Field trips and lab activities
  • User testing with Shanghai partners
  • Partnered design with local schools and organizations
  • Showcase of projects with educators, technologists, and musicians

设计者思考的问题 / Design Questions

  • 你想要在这些apps里听到什么样的音乐？ / What music would you like to see in these apps?
  • 这些apps可以怎样更好地适用于中国的音乐教育环境？ / How might these be adapted for a Chinese context?
  • 使用这些apps的过程中遇到了哪些障碍？ / What are some barriers to using these apps?
  • 这些apps为老师、学生、家长创造了哪些新机会？ / What are some opportunities these apps enable?
