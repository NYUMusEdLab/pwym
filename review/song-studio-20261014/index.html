Automatic help: what is available?

Song Studio does not automatically transcribe your song. The separate timing-service pilot aligns supplied English lyrics and returns reviewable timing JSON. The Fresh Annotator example includes existing lyric timings. Sketch suggestions use 22 local picture templates and an English word list, with manual picture choice for other words.

Rough word positions use audio energy and word count. They do not recognize vocals or words. Section suggestions use quiet gaps; you choose labels like verse or chorus.

For model assistance, a grown-up can run the optional offline Whisper script with an already downloaded model, then import its JSON here. Singing, repeated words, backing voices, and instruments all need a careful listen.
